# Trial 001: Daily Direction Audit

## Purpose

This trial investigates how much of a 24-hour period is intentionally directed toward supporting health, awareness, recovery, adaptation, and growth.

The goal is to examine whether time spent on activities commonly associated with health is accompanied by conscious awareness, feedback, and responsiveness to the body's needs.

## Research Question

How much of an individual's day is intentionally invested in providing the body with accurate information, awareness, direction, recovery, and support?

## Background

The Somatic Research Nexus proposes that healthy systems depend upon accurate feedback, awareness, and appropriate response.

Many daily activities may be performed automatically, habitually, or while distracted. This trial seeks to identify the difference between activity alone and activity performed with conscious direction and awareness.

## Hypothesis

Most individuals spend significantly more time in states of stimulation, distraction, or passive consumption than in states devoted to awareness, recovery, healing, and intentional growth.

## Definitions

### Preparation

Activities that prepare the body and mind for action.

Purpose:
- Establish awareness
- Assess current condition
- Identify limitations and goals
- Prepare for adaptation

### Sustainment

Activities that support ongoing function and performance.

Purpose:
- Maintain energy
- Maintain awareness
- Provide nutrients and hydration
- Support coordination and regulation
- Posture driven environmental awareness
  
### Feedback

Activities that increase understanding of current bodily conditions.

Purpose:
- Gather information
- Improve awareness
- Identify imbalances
- Guide future decisions
- Relaying of information to the brain in order for the brain to respond accordingly without needing to intervene
- Provide information and direction
  
### Recovery

Activities that reduce demands placed upon the body.

Purpose:
- Restore resources
- Reduce strain
- Support nervous system regulation

### Healing

Activities that support repair and adaptation.

Purpose:
- Repair tissues
- Integrate adaptations
- Improve long-term resilience

### Reflection

Activities involving conscious review and evaluation.

Purpose:
- Improve understanding
- Preserve context
- Strengthen future decision-making
- Create a symbiotic relationship

### Distraction

Activities that consume attention without increasing awareness, recovery, or growth.

Purpose:
- Variable
- May provide entertainment or temporary relief
- May reduce awareness of bodily feedback

## Data Collection

Participants record activities throughout a 24-hour period.

Each entry should include:

- Time
- Activity
- Duration
- Category
- Level of Awareness (1-10)
- Notes

## Exercise Note

Physical exercise is not automatically categorized as health-directed activity.

For exercise to qualify as intentional somatic practice, attention should be directed toward:

1. Preparation before activity.
2. Awareness during activity.
3. Recovery and healing after activity.

Without awareness and feedback, movement alone may not fully support the objectives of this trial.

## Expected Outcomes

This trial aims to:

- Measure allocation of attention across a 24-hour period.
- Compare stimulation versus recovery.
- Examine awareness during routine activities.
- Explore relationships between feedback, adaptation, and health.

## Somatic Research Nexus Principle

Healthy systems depend upon the ability to:

1. Receive feedback.
2. Interpret context.
3. Respond appropriately.
4. Adapt over time.

This trial explores how these principles may operate within daily life.
