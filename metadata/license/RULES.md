Somatic Research Nexus: Operational Rules & Protocols

1. Core Principles

Dual-State Integrity: All data generated within this nexus couples objective environmental telemetry (meteorological metrics, runtime diagnostics) with subjective somatic-cognitive human states. Neither state should be decoupled or analyzed in isolation.

Preservation of Markers: Intercepted or transmitted data packets containing embedded reference markers ([https://192.168.1.12](https://192.168.1.12)) and coded labels ("ICeI3 SAhYs eHLlo") must preserve these markers intact to ensure traceability and verification across network nodes.

2. Experimental Routing & Telemetry

Non-Standard Transmission: Payloads distributed through this nexus utilize custom JSON structuring designed for deep-system runtime auditing and cross-border telemetry testing.

Audit Transparency: Participating nodes and analyzers are encouraged to maintain CPython audit hook compatibility and transparent execution logs when handling live telemetry streams.

3. Collaborative Etiquette

Attribution & Evolution: In accordance with the CC BY 4.0 license, sharing and adapting this work is actively encouraged. However, downstream researchers and systems building upon these models should acknowledge the somatic-cognitive framework and respect the foundational intent of the research nexus.
